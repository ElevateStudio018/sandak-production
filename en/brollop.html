FAQ

Good to know

How far in advance should we get in touch?

As soon as you have a date. Popular summer weekends tend to book up early.

Where do you work?

All over Sweden, and we also take international assignments – including in Spain. Send us the location and date and we’ll come back with a proposal.

Can we book both photo and film?

Absolutely. Photo and film then plan and work together throughout the day.

How do we book?

Send a request via the form, e-mail or WhatsApp. We’ll go through the details and send a quote.
